# Post-Purchase Quick-Start & Review Email

Buyers who succeed in the first 5 minutes leave reviews — and reviews are what rank you early.
This is a reusable template for **all four products**. Fill the **[brackets]**.

### Where each channel lets you send it
- **Gumroad / Payhip:** you can email buyers. Paste **Email 1** into the product's "Receipt /
  content" note (sent instantly) and schedule **Email 2** as a buyer broadcast a few days later.
- **Etsy:** you may **not** email buyers off-platform or incentivize reviews. Instead: (a) put
  **Email 1's content as a "Read Me First" PDF inside the download**, and (b) send a brief,
  no-incentive thank-you via **Etsy Messages**. Keep Etsy review asks soft and compliant.

Per-product fill-ins (the `[Quick steps]` block) are at the bottom.

## PER-PRODUCT `[Quick steps]` BLOCK

**Short-Term Rental Tracker** (Schedule E)
> 2. On the **Setup** tab, type your property names. Then on **Transactions**, log each booking and expense — pick the property, choose Income/Expense, pick a category. Remember: log the gross payout as income and the platform fee as a separate expense.

**Rental Property Tracker** (Schedule E)
> 2. On the **Setup** tab, type your property addresses. Then on **Transactions**, log rent and expenses — pick the property, choose Income/Expense, pick a category. Remember: only the *interest* part of your mortgage is an expense, and a security deposit isn't income unless you keep it.

**Online Seller Bookkeeping Tracker** (Schedule C)
> 2. On the **Setup** tab, type your shop names (Etsy, Amazon, etc.). Then on **Transactions**, log sales and costs — pick the shop, choose Income/Expense, pick a category. Remember: log gross sales as income and the marketplace fee as a separate expense, and put materials under Cost of Goods Sold.

**Freelancer & Self-Employed Tracker** (Schedule C)
> 2. On the **Setup** tab, add your clients plus the "(General / overhead)" bucket. Then on **Transactions**, log invoices and expenses — tag each to a client (or to overhead), choose Income/Expense, pick a category. Tip: the Dashboard shows profit per client, and a good rule of thumb is to set aside ~25–30% of profit for quarterly taxes.

**Real Estate Agent Tracker** (Schedule C)
> 2. On the **Setup** tab, add your active deals plus the "(General / business)" bucket. Then on **Transactions**, log commissions and expenses — tag each to a deal (or to general business), choose Income/Expense, pick a category. Remember: log your gross commission as income and your brokerage split as a separate expense, and track every business mile.
