# How to List Your Products — Complete Beginner's Guide

You've never done this before — that's fine. This walks you through **every click**. The hard
parts (the products, the photos, the video, and all the words) are **already done for you** and
sitting in this repo. Your job is mostly **upload and paste**.

> ⏱️ **Time:** ~20–30 minutes for your first product on Gumroad, ~30–40 on Etsy. Faster after that.

---

## Step 0 — Decide where to sell (do both, in this order)

1. **Gumroad** (or Payhip) — **start here.** Easiest possible setup, you keep the buyer's email,
   and there's **no listing fee**. Great for your very first listing and learning the ropes.
2. **Etsy** — add this next. It has **built-in shoppers searching**, which is where most of your
   early sales will come from. Costs $0.20 per listing + ~6.5% + payment fees per sale.

Pick a **store name** first (ideas in `STOREFRONT.md`) and use it on both.

# PART A — List on Gumroad (easiest, start here)

### A1. Create your account
1. Go to **gumroad.com** → **Start selling** → sign up (email or Google).
2. Set your profile name to your **store name**. Add a one-line bio (copy the "Store bio" from `STOREFRONT.md`).
3. When asked for payout info, connect your bank or PayPal (you can do this later, before your first payout).

### A2. Create the product
1. Click **New product** → choose **Digital product**.
2. **Name:** paste the product name (e.g. "Online Seller Bookkeeping Tracker"). You can shorten the long Etsy title here.
3. **Price:** type the launch price from `listing-copy.md` (usually **19**).
4. Click **Next: Customize**.

### A3. Upload the files
1. In the product editor, find **Content** / **Files** → **Upload files**.
2. Upload **two** files from the product folder: the **`.xlsx`** and the **`Setup-Guide.pdf`**.
3. (Optional, recommended) Also upload the "Read Me First" PDF if you make one from `QUICKSTART-EMAIL.md`.

### A4. Add the photos and video
1. Find the **Cover / Thumbnail** area.
2. Upload all **5 images** from the product's `images/` folder. Drag **`01-hero.png`** to be **first** — that's your thumbnail.
3. Upload **`preview-video.mp4`** as a cover video if Gumroad offers it (it shows alongside the images).

### A5. Add the description
1. In the **Description** box, paste the **DESCRIPTION** section from `listing-copy.md`.
2. Keep the disclaimer line at the bottom (it protects you).
3. (Optional) In the product's **Summary** field, paste the **GUMROAD SHORT BLURB**.

### A6. Set up the thank-you / receipt email
1. Find **Content** → **Receipt** (or "Email after purchase").
2. Paste **Email 1** from `QUICKSTART-EMAIL.md` (the 5-minute quick-start). Swap in this product's `[Quick steps]` block.
3. This is what makes buyers succeed fast → which gets you reviews.

### A7. Publish
1. Click **Publish** (top right). Done — you have a live product with a shareable link.
2. Copy the link; you'll use it to share and for the "review" email later.

✅ **That's a full listing.** Repeat A2–A7 for each product (it gets fast).

# PART B — List on Etsy (where the shoppers are)

### B1. Create your shop
1. Go to **etsy.com/sell** → **Open your Etsy shop** → sign up.
2. Set shop language/country/currency → **Shop name** (your store name).
3. Etsy will ask how you'll get paid (Etsy Payments → your bank) and to add a billing card (for the listing fees).

### B2. Start a listing
1. Shop Manager → **Listings** → **Add a listing**.

### B3. Add photos and video
1. **Photos:** upload all **5 images** from the product's `images/` folder. Put **`01-hero.png` first** (Etsy uses the first photo as the thumbnail).
   - Etsy lets you add up to 10 photos; 5 is plenty.
2. **Video:** upload **`preview-video.mp4`** (Etsy allows one 5–15s video; ours is ~14s ✓). No sound needed.

### B4. Fill in the listing details (copy-paste from `listing-copy.md`)
1. **Title:** paste **Title option #1**.
2. **About this listing:** "Who made it" = **I did**; "What is it" = **A finished product**; "When" = **Made to order** or a recent year (either is fine for digital).
3. **Category:** type "template" → choose a Templates category.
4. **Type:** select **Digital**.
5. **Description:** paste the **DESCRIPTION** from `listing-copy.md`. Keep the disclaimer.
6. **Tags:** paste all **13 tags** from `listing-copy.md` (one per tag box).
7. **Materials/attributes:** optional.

### B5. Set price and files
1. **Price:** the launch price from `listing-copy.md` (e.g. 19; realtor 24).
2. **Quantity:** for digital it can stay 1 (Etsy re-stocks digital automatically).
3. **Digital files:** upload the **`.xlsx`** and the **`Setup-Guide.pdf`** (Etsy allows up to 5 files).
   - (Optional) include the "Read Me First" PDF here, since Etsy won't let you email buyers off-platform.

### B6. The AI-disclosure rule (don't skip — it's 2026 Etsy policy)
1. In **"Who made it,"** Etsy treats AI-assisted work as **"Designed by"** you (a member of your shop), not "Made by" a third party — that's correct here.
2. The description already contains the required **AI-disclosure sentence** — leave it in.
3. Do **not** ask for reviews in exchange for anything — Etsy bans incentivized reviews.

### B7. Publish
1. Click **Publish** (this is where the $0.20 listing fee applies).
2. Your listing is live and will start showing in Etsy search.

✅ Repeat B2–B7 for each product. **Tip:** use Etsy's **"Copy"** on an existing listing to clone the layout, then just swap the files, photos, title, tags, and description.

## Optional: tweak a photo in Canva (you don't have to)
If you ever want to change a headline or add your logo:
1. Go to **canva.com** (free) → **Create a design** → **Custom size 2000×2000 px**.
2. **Upload** the image you want from the product's `images/` folder, drop it on the canvas.
3. Add/replace text, drag your logo on, then **Share → Download → PNG**. Re-upload it to the listing.

That's it — but again, the provided images are ready to use as-is.

## Quick FAQ

**Do I need a business / LLC to start?** No. You can sell as an individual; report income at tax time. (Confirm with a tax pro.)

**Which product should I list first?** Whichever keyword looked best in eRank. If unsure, **Online Seller** (huge search volume) or **Real Estate Agent** (highest price). See `LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`.

**The numbers in the photos — are those real?** They're the example data that ships *inside* the product, so they're accurate to what the buyer sees. Totally fine to show.

**How fast will I sell?** Often the first sale takes ~30–60 days; expect a few hundred $/month building over ~6 months as reviews and listings stack. Steady, not overnight.

**Can you send me a specific product's files in chat?** Yes — just ask and I'll deliver the .xlsx, PDF, all 5 images, and the video for any product.
