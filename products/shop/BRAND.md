# Brand Guide — LedgerLite

The shop's identity. Everything visual is generated by `../build_brand.py` — change the
`BRAND` / `TAGLINE` variables at the top of that file and re-run to rebrand in one step.

## Name & tagline
- **Name:** **LedgerLite**
- **Tagline:** *Bookkeeping that does your taxes for you*
- **Sub-tagline:** *Spreadsheet trackers for hosts, sellers & the self-employed*

> ⚠️ **Before you finalize:** confirm the name is free where it matters —
> (1) Etsy shop name (must be unique; Etsy will tell you at signup),
> (2) Gumroad/Payhip URL handle, (3) ideally a matching domain (ledgerlite.co / .shop)
> and the Instagram/TikTok handle. If LedgerLite is taken, pick an alternate below and
> re-run `build_brand.py` — all assets regenerate automatically.

**Alternate names (same vibe):** Ledgerly · TidyLedger · PlainSheet · TidyBooks Co. ·
ProfitSheet · SheetSet · PennyLedger · The Sheet Shop

## Logo & mark
- The mark = a rounded white "app tile" with **three rising bars** (growth/profit) and a
  **teal check badge** (tax-ready / done).
- Use **`logo-on-white.png`** on light backgrounds, **`logo-on-navy.png`** on dark.
- Use **`icon-500.png`** as the Etsy shop icon and Gumroad avatar (square).
- Clear space: keep at least half the mark's width of padding around the logo. Don't
  recolor, stretch, or add effects.

This is the same palette as every product image, so the whole shop looks cohesive.

## Typography
- Headlines/wordmark: a clean bold sans (assets use DejaVu Sans Bold; **Poppins**,
  **Montserrat**, or **Inter** are great free web equivalents in Canva/Etsy text).
- Body: the regular weight of the same family.

## Voice
Plain-English, friendly, reassuring, never jargon-y. We sell *relief from a chore*. Lead with
the outcome ("walk into tax season ready"), not features. Confident but honest — never promise
specific income or give tax advice.
