## About / Shop story (Etsy "About" section · Gumroad profile)

**Headline:** Bookkeeping shouldn't need an accountant — or a monthly subscription.

Most bookkeeping tools are either a shoebox of receipts or a $30-a-month app you'll never fully use. LedgerLite is the in-between you actually wanted: one clean spreadsheet, built for *your* kind of work, that sorts every dollar onto the right tax line and shows your real profit.

You log what comes in and what goes out. The spreadsheet does the rest — a tax-ready IRS summary and a live dashboard, by client/property/shop and by month. Hand it to your accountant, or copy the totals into your tax software.

Every template:
- **Works in Excel, Google Sheets & Apple Numbers** — no new software to learn
- Is a **one-time purchase** — yours forever, reuse it every year, no subscription
- Comes **pre-filled with an example** so you can see exactly how it works
- Ships with a **step-by-step setup guide** — you'll be running in under 10 minutes

Built for people who'd rather spend their time *running* the business than wrestling a spreadsheet.

*LedgerLite templates are bookkeeping organizers, not tax, legal, or accounting advice — please confirm your specific filing with a qualified professional. Designed in-house with the help of AI tools.*

## Shop policies

**Digital / instant delivery**
> All items are digital downloads. Your files are available immediately after your purchase is confirmed — nothing is shipped. You'll get the spreadsheet (.xlsx) plus a setup-guide PDF.

**Returns, refunds & exchanges**
> Because these are instant digital downloads, all sales are final and we don't offer returns or exchanges. But your happiness matters — if a file won't open, something looks wrong, or you're stuck, **message me** and I'll make it right (fix, re-send, or help you set it up).

**Cancellations**
> Orders can't be cancelled once the files have been downloaded.

**How to use your files**
> Open the .xlsx in Excel or Apple Numbers, or upload it to Google Sheets (Drive → New → File upload → open → Save as Google Sheets). The included PDF guide walks you through setup step by step.

**Privacy**
> Your spreadsheet stays on your computer / your Google account — your financial data never passes through me.

**Not tax advice**
> LedgerLite templates are bookkeeping organizers to help you stay organized. They are not tax, legal, or accounting advice; the IRS line mapping is for convenience only. Please confirm your specific situation with a qualified tax professional.
