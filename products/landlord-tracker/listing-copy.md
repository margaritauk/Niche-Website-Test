**Own rental property? Get your books — and your taxes — under control with one simple spreadsheet.**

Stop juggling bank statements, repair receipts, and that mortgage 1098 every April. This tracker turns your rental income and expenses into a clean, tax-ready **IRS Schedule E** summary — automatically. Log it once; the spreadsheet does the categorizing, the totals, and the math.

**★ WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU ★**
✔️ Auto-builds your **IRS Schedule E** summary — every expense mapped to the right line, per property
✔️ Tracks up to **5 properties / units** side by side
✔️ A live **dashboard**: net cash flow, income vs. expenses, totals by property and by month
✔️ Built-in **dropdown categories** (rent, repairs, mortgage interest, property tax, insurance, management fees, HOA…) so your books stay consistent
✔️ Handles the things landlords get wrong: security deposits that aren't income, and mortgage **interest** vs. principal
✔️ Hand it straight to your accountant — or copy the totals into your tax software

**★ WHY LANDLORDS LOVE IT ★**
• **One-time purchase. No subscription, ever.** Reuse it every year, for every unit.
• Works in **Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers**
• **Pre-filled example data** shows you exactly how it works — then delete and start
• Set up in **under 10 minutes** with the included step-by-step PDF guide
• No accounting background needed

**★ WHAT YOU GET (instant download) ★**
