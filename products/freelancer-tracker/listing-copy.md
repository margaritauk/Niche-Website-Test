**Freelancing is the fun part. The bookkeeping doesn't have to be the nightmare part.**

If you're a freelancer, 1099 contractor, or run a solo service business, this spreadsheet turns your invoices and expenses into a clean, tax-ready **IRS Schedule C** summary — automatically. ✔️ Built-in **dropdown categories** — software, subcontractors, travel, fees, supplies & more
✔️ A clean **(General / overhead)** bucket so shared costs don't get pinned to one client
✔️ Know your profit so you can **set aside the right amount for quarterly taxes**

**★ WHY FREELANCERS LOVE IT ★**
• **One-time purchase. No subscription, ever.** Reuse it every year.
• Works in **Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers**

• Set up in **under 10 minutes** with the included step-by-step PDF guide

**★ WHAT YOU GET (instant download) ★**
