# AI Business Opportunity — Decision Record

**Date:** 2026-06-06
**Constraints given:** no domain/skill edge (pick on data), only a few hours of upfront
time, fully AI-generatable, instant digital delivery, as hands-off as possible, fastest
path to *steady* income with high probability of taking off, low budget.

## Decision

**Build a niche functional spreadsheet template — a "Short-Term Rental (Airbnb/VRBO)
Bookkeeping & Tax Tracker" (Google Sheets / Excel)** — sold as an instant download on
**Gumroad/Payhip (owned store + email) + Etsy (discovery)**. Launch at **$19**, raise to
**$24–29** after reviews. See `products/str-tracker/`.

## Why this, over the alternatives

Three parallel market-validation passes (coloring/printable art, functional templates,
emerging 2026 micro-niches) converged on one rule: **saturation is about specificity, not
category** — the same product re-aimed at a specific, vocal, spending audience is wide open.

The short-term-rental tracker scored best against the exact constraints:

| Criterion | Why it wins |
|-----------|-------------|
| No domain edge needed | AI supplies the entire domain layer (Schedule E lines, formulas, structure). |
| A few hours to produce | One spreadsheet; AI generates formulas + copy. |
| Fastest *steady* money | High price ($19–40) + tax-deductible, price-insensitive buyers → few sales needed. |
| Defensible | Formulas/structure are genuine human-authored work. |
| Hands-off | Instant digital delivery; no clients or support at small scale. |
| Demand is real | Live 2026 listings with 857+ favorites; fresh listings every month. |
| Niche is open | Generic "rental tracker" is contested; the **STR/Airbnb-specific** variant is not. |

### Two validated runner-ups (good second products)
- **Condition-specific symptom tracker (printable PDF)** — POTS/endometriosis/fibromyalgia;
  lowest effort, 1,000+ favorites on live listings, underserved at single-condition level.
  Avoid medical-claim language.
- **Care-driven coloring books** — dementia/senior large-print or grief/pet-loss; thin
  competition, price-insensitive gift buyers, fastest AI production. Weaker defensibility
  (see legal note), so a volume play rather than the core.

## Two 2026 developments that shaped the choice (and rule out the "easy" AI plays)

1. **Thaler v. Perlmutter (Supreme Court, Mar 2026):** purely AI-generated *images* with no
   meaningful human authorship get **no copyright** — competitors can legally copy/resell
   them. ➡️ A spreadsheet's formulas/structure are human-authored → defensible. This is the
   main reason we chose a functional product over AI coloring/wall-art.
2. **Etsy mandatory AI disclosure (2026):** AI-assisted listings must disclose and list
   under "Designed by." ➡️ Easy to comply (disclosure line is in the listing copy); functional
   templates draw far less enforcement scrutiny than AI image floods.
